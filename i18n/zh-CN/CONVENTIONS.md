# zh-CN Conventions

Language-specific conventions for the Simplified Chinese localization. The general rules for all locales live in [`LOCALIZATION.md`](../../LOCALIZATION.md).

- Reader address: use **你**; keep a concise technical register.
- Do not localize product names (CUDA, vLLM, TensorRT, MAX, SGLang).
- "KV cache" stays in English on first mention; `KV 缓存` afterwards is fine.
- Use full-width punctuation for Chinese prose, and a space between CJK text and Latin words or numbers.
